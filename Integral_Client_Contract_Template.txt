2. SCOPE OF LICENCE AND SERVICES
================================================================================

2.1 Licence. Integral grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Software at the
    Facility for internal healthcare operations, subject to this Agreement and full payment of fees.

2.2 Services. Integral shall supply, install/configure, train, and commission the Software according to the
    Quotation / Schedule, which may include:
    (a) base HMIS licence and agreed modules (clinical, billing, pharmacy/stores, HR/payroll, finance, etc.);
    (b) national / third-party integrations (e.g. SHA, DHA-related rails, Smart, M-Pesa, fiscalization);
    (c) implementation, parameterization, and agreed customizations;
    (d) role-based user and administrator training; and
    (e) go-live support and post-go-live stabilization as specified.

2.3 Out of scope. Items not listed in the Quotation are excluded, including hardware supply (unless quoted),
    structured cabling, internet service, third-party licence fees payable to regulators/vendors, and major
    custom development unless approved under Change Control.

3. IMPLEMENTATION METHODOLOGY AND TIMELINE
================================================================================

3.1 Indicative phases (refined per project plan):
    (1) Requirements confirmation, workflow review and scoping / SRS sign-off;
    (2) Environment setup and foundational configuration;
    (3) Master data setup (services, charges, stock, users, payer mappings);
    (4) Workflow configuration and approved integrations;
    (5) Testing, UAT and issue closure / agreed workarounds;
    (6) Role-based training and go-live readiness;
    (7) Go-live, stabilization and handover to support.

3.2 Go-Live readiness criteria typically include: approved workflows and roles; completed UAT (or agreed
    deferrals); completed agreed training; infrastructure readiness; and nominated support contacts.

3.3 Assumptions. Timelines depend on timely Client inputs, approvals, stakeholder availability,
    infrastructure readiness, and third-party access. Delays attributable to the Client or third parties may
    shift milestones and commercial assumptions.

3.4 Warranty support. Unless otherwise agreed, Integral provides ninety (90) days post-Go-Live warranty
    support for material defects in the agreed scope (excluding Client misuse, unsupported environments,
    and third-party faults).

5. DATA MIGRATION, THIRD PARTIES AND CHANGE CONTROL
================================================================================

5.1 Data migration from legacy systems is excluded unless expressly priced and agreed in writing. Where
    included, the Client remains responsible for quality, completeness, legality and correctness of source
    data. Integral is not obliged to cleanse or reconstruct historical data beyond agreed import mapping.

5.2 Third-party systems (SHA, DHA/HIE, banks, SMS, device OEMs, payment rails, hosting providers) remain
    outside Integral’s sole control. Integral will use reasonable efforts to enable agreed integrations, but
    does not guarantee third-party availability, approvals, pricing, performance or policy stability.
    Claim acceptance and reimbursement outcomes remain subject to Client data quality, clinical/billing
    practice, payer rules and external platform availability.

5.3 Change Control. After baseline confirmation, any change to scope, workflows, integrations, reports,
    training model, deployment model or timelines is a Change Request and requires written approval,
    with assessment of feasibility, timeline and commercial impact. Product enhancements for shared
    platforms may be routed through Integral’s roadmap/release process rather than immediate
    project change.
